dated record of how it got there, like every other file in this folder.

## 2. The vision (approved content, written into `PRODUCT.md` in PR 1)

**Horizon:** through the Bagrut season, June 2027.

- **Phase A (now to 2027-03-31):** a lifestyle business. Two tutors, full
  calendars, and the software does the prep.
- **Phase B:** the brand grows beyond two tutors. It starts on a trigger,
  not a date. The trigger is all three of: both calendars full, prep at 10
  minutes or less per lesson, and families waiting. The board says no more
  than that about phase B, since anything further would be a guess.

**North stars, ranked:**
1. Nikol's prep time. A feature that adds a manual step for her is a net
   loss.
2. A parent sees evidence of progress without having to ask.

**Targets for 2027-03-31:**

| Target | Measured by |
|---|---|
| 10 active students | ≥1 completed lesson in the last 30 days |
| Prep time from 60 to 10 min per lesson | strict autogenerated share, plus a monthly check-in with Nikol (no timer, no manual logging) |
| 80% of lessons and homework autogenerated | `lesson_materials.origin`. **Strict:** published with no `edited` version. **Lenient:** generated first, edits allowed, reported next to strict |
| ₪10k a month, about ₪1,000 per family | `payments`; the mechanism is steering families to כפול (90 min a week) |

*Amended 2026-09-24 after the final review: generation never writes `lesson_materials` (only the tutor's editor does), so the autogenerated share is measured over generation runs in the legacy `lessons` table — strict = `ready` with no `edited`/`restored` material; lenient = `ready` or `held` — plus generation-runs-vs-lessons-taught coverage. `restored` counts as tutor intervention.*

**Pillars, in priority order:**

| # | Pillar | Todoist label |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lesson autopilot | `pillar-autopilot` |
| 2 | Progress parents can see | `pillar-progress` |
| 3 | Word of mouth | `pillar-wom` |
| 4 | Ninety minutes by default | `pillar-90min` |
| 5 | Ready for a third tutor | `pillar-tutor3` |
| floor | Reliability (backup, deploy, calendar, mail) | `@Infra` |

**Gate:** no new features outside the pillars until Codex lesson generation
runs end-to-end in production for 10 real lessons in a row. This replaces
PRD §2's "improve the existing, no new features until the foundations are
stable" with something that can be checked.

**Non-goals for this horizon:** automatic payment collection; a marketplace
or tutor choice at booking; subjects other than math; a native app or PWA;
the WhatsApp API for families; selling the platform to other tutors.

**Decision authority:** Lior owns the business and settles every decision,
including teaching, design and marketing. Nothing is parked as "pending
Nikol."

## 4. PR 2: `scripts/vision-metrics.mjs`

A read-only command-line script, written test-first. It opens the SQLite
database under `DATA_DIR` and prints:
- active students
- strict and lenient autogenerated share over the last 30 days
- revenue for the last calendar month
- the current streak of clean Codex runs (for the gate)

The test is `tests/unit/vision-metrics.test.mjs`, run against a fixture
database. To confirm while writing it: the real table and column names,
where generation runs and their outcomes are recorded (if the streak can't
be derived, the script says so and doesn't guess), and whether `scripts/`
ships in the Docker image. One line in `server/README.md` says how to run
it. No UI.

*Amended 2026-09-24 after the final review: generation never writes `lesson_materials` (only the tutor's editor does), so the autogenerated share is measured over generation runs in the legacy `lessons` table — strict = `ready` with no `edited`/`restored` material; lenient = `ready` or `held` — plus generation-runs-vs-lessons-taught coverage. `restored` counts as tutor intervention.*

## 6. The board

A static artifact, RTL and in Hebrew, private with a shareable link, in the
site's own brand colors and fonts, working at phone width and in dark mode.
It has no runtime features. Sections:
- header and phase timeline
- the two north stars
- four target cards (target, how measured, baseline)
- the 0/10 gate bar
- five pillar cards, each listing its tasks as Now / Next / Later
  (p1 / p2 / p3–p4, derived and never hand-sorted), with the reliability floor
  as a band underneath
- "לא עושים" (not doing)
- footer: "generated from `PRODUCT.md` @ `<commit>` and Todoist on `<date>`"

Baselines come from the first production run of `vision-metrics.mjs`.
Until then the card says "measured at the first check-in" and shows no
number. The board is regenerated (same URL) whenever the PRD or Todoist
changes. It is never edited by hand.

the post-merge run is checked each time. Neither PR changes runtime
behavior of the site.
