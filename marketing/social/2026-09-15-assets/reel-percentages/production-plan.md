## Reel idea

## Phone filming instructions

1. Use a vertical phone mount directly above a light desk near a window.
2. Prepare cards marked `240`, `24`, `12`, `36`, `80`, `10%`, `5%`, `15%` in navy, pale blue and orange.
3. Record one continuous 25-second take at 30 fps. Move the cards by hand and pause briefly at every result.
4. Keep hands and all text inside the central 80% of the frame so Instagram controls do not cover them.
5. Cut only the pauses; use two quick match cuts when 240 becomes 24 and when 24 splits into 12.
6. Add a soft paper-slide sound, one click when the pieces form 36, and light instrumental music without lyrics under the voiceover.
7. Export H.264 MP4, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Check the entire video once with sound and once muted.

## Captions and accessibility

- Use large navy Hebrew subtitles on a warm cream translucent paper strip.
- Keep each subtitle to one short line when possible.
- Burn the subtitles into the video so the explanation works without sound.
- Do not place text in the bottom 20% or top 12% of the frame.
